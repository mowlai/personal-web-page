This work focused on applying reinforcement learning to intelligent building
energy control. The objective was to learn control policies that could balance
energy use with operational requirements in a dynamic environment.

## Policy development

I developed **Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)** policies in simulation,
working across state and action design, reward formulation, training, and policy
evaluation. The system treated building control as a sequential decision-making
problem in which present actions affect both immediate consumption and future
system behavior.

## Evaluation

Before deployment, candidate policies were evaluated offline against simulated
and historical operating behavior. This made it possible to compare policies,
inspect failure modes, and assess whether improvements remained consistent
across operating conditions.

## Production lifecycle

The work extended beyond model training to AWS deployment and production
monitoring. The resulting workflow connected experimentation, policy validation,
deployment, and ongoing observation of system behavior.
