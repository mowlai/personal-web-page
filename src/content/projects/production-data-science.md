This work centered on production data science systems for behavioral analytics,
anomaly detection, and operational monitoring. The focus was turning raw,
heterogeneous data into reliable signals that could support day-to-day
decisions.

## Data and feature pipelines

I worked across ETL, data validation, and feature engineering to create
analysis-ready datasets for downstream models and monitoring. These pipelines
provided a consistent foundation for both exploratory analysis and recurring
production workflows.

## Predictive analytics

I developed predictive approaches for behavioral analysis and anomaly
detection, translating observed patterns into indicators that could be tracked
over time. The work included model development as well as the surrounding
analytics needed to interpret outputs in an operational context.

## Operational monitoring

The final layer connected model and pipeline outputs to production analytics,
making unusual behavior and changes in key signals visible for investigation.
